About

Everyone knows the first fall. It happens to you. The second one you choose.

You walk in, the doors close behind you, and the night takes over from there.

The Fanglle II is built around that choice. One room, one sound system, four nights a week, and a door that opens at 3 pm whether the street outside is ready or not.

House rules

  • 21 and overValid ID for everyone, every night. No exceptions for tables.
  • Smart dressNo sportswear, no flip-flops, no swimwear.
  • One QR, one entryEach QR works once. Screenshots shared with friends will not get them in.
  • Tables have a minimum spendYour deposit counts toward it. The rest is spent on the night.

Private nights

The room can be booked for private nights from Monday to Wednesday. Tell us the date, the number of guests, and what you have in mind.
